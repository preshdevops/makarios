# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Believers, Christians, and individuals seeking intentional spiritual presence, biblical anchoring, and peace amidst daily anxiety, burdens, transitions, and mental fatigue. They access the app during moments of vulnerability (early morning dawn, mid-day overwhelm, evening reflection).

## Product Purpose

Makarios exists to transform daily burdens into sovereign biblical declarations through ambient presence. It provides:
1. **"Speak into Truth"**: A prompt-driven reflection input where users articulate what they carry (doubt, fear, fatigue, direction), distilling it into an affirmative, first-person biblical declaration and grounded scripture context.
2. **"Everyday Presence"**: Ambient Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets (Small 2×2, Medium 4×2, Large 4×4) that surface curated biblical truths across customizable cadences (Every Dawn, Every 4h, On Wake).
3. **Wallpaper & Creative Studio**: High-res, editorial wallpapers for phone lock screens and social sharing.

## Positioning

"Gospel. Tech. Precious." — An intentional rejection of cold SaaS templates and loud notifications. Makarios positions itself as a sacred, peaceful digital companion with museum-grade typography (Fraunces & Work Sans) and warm, sunlit Japandi aesthetics.

## Operating Context

- **Dawn & Morning Rhythms**: First phone check upon waking; glanceable reassurance on the Lock Screen.
- **Mid-Day Focus**: Quick affirmation retrieval when anxiety strikes.
- **Evening Examen**: Reflective, quiet wind-down before sleep.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Cross-Platform Delivery**: React Native / Expo core with native platform targets:
  - iOS WidgetKit (Swift / SwiftUI) for native Lock Screen & Home Screen widgets.
  - Android Jetpack Glance (Kotlin / Compose) for native home screen widgets.
- **Offline Reliability**: Curated affirmations and fallback tracks stored locally on device.
- **Visual Faithfulness**: Absolute pixel-level fidelity to the core design mockup (`media_1789142425641.png`).

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: Makarios (Greek: μακάριος — blessed, spiritually joyful, deeply fulfilled).
- **Voice**: Resolute, gentle, still, sovereign.
- **Palette**: Warm porcelain (`#FAF8F5`), deep espresso-plum (`#1F1118`), warm almond/sand (`#EAE3D8`), and terracotta salmon (`#C46851`).
- **Typography**: Fraunces (variable optical display serif) and Work Sans (clean humanist sans).

## Product Principles

1. **Peace Before Metrics**: No streaks, guilt mechanics, or gamified badges.
2. **One Signature Move**: Calm, high-fidelity typography and organic light over stacked decorative gimmicks.
3. **Ambient Presence**: Truth meets the user where they already glance (Lock Screen, Home Screen) rather than demanding endless in-app screen time.
4. **Immediate Grounding**: Any burden entered can be distilled into scripture within seconds.
